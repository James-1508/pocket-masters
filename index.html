🎱 Pocket Masters Guide & Rules

🎮 Controls

  • Aim Cue: Move your mouse around the cue ball.
  • Fine Tune Aim: Use Left / Right arrow keys or A / D.
  • Power & Shoot: Drag the cue stick backward and release, OR use the power slider and click Strike (or press Space).
  • Toggle Aim Line: Press L or click the Aim Line toggle button to show/hide the target ball deflection trajectory!
  • Ball in Hand: When fouled, click anywhere on the felt to reposition the cue ball.
  • English / Spin: Click on the Cue Ball Spin widget to add topspin (follow), backspin (draw), or sidespin.
  • Cloth Speed & Friction: Switch between Realistic, Slick, or Heavy felt anytime using the Cloth dropdown in the bottom toolbar.

📜 8-Ball Rules

  • Break Shot: Player 1 breaks from behind the head string. Table remains open until a ball is pocketed.
  • Solids (1-7) vs Stripes (9-15): The first player to legally pocket a ball claims that suit.
  • Legal Shot: Cue ball must hit your suit first, and at least one ball must hit a cushion or be pocketed.
  • Fouls: Scratching, hitting wrong suit first, or missing all balls grants opponent Ball in Hand anywhere on the table.
  • The 8-Ball: Once your suit is cleared, legally pocket the 8-ball to WIN. Pocketing the 8-ball early or scratching on the 8-ball is an automatic LOSS.
